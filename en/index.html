Services

Consulting

MB-Water provides consulting services in the fields of water resources management and contaminated land management. MB-Water brings strong expertise in groundwater, both for technical assessments and policy development.

Research

MB-Water conducts research related to water, climate and environmental topics for a wide variety of sectors. Whenever possible, results are published and made publicly available.

Project management

MB-Water supports organisations with scoping and management of water and environmental projects. This starts by developing the right project goals and strategy and ends with implementing project outcomes that have impact.

About MB-Water
MATTHIJS BONTE

MB-Water is the independent consulting company of Matthijs Bonte, PhD. Matthijs is a hydrologist with a broad range of experience including the Dutch national government (Ministry of Infrastructure and Water Management), the energy sector (Shell Global Solutions), scientific research (KWR-Water, VU University Amsterdam) and consulting (Witteveen+Bos).

The experience includes groundwater management, water resources and drought resilience, contaminated land management and environmental and climate related issues.

Matthijs obtained MSc and PhD degrees from the VU University Amsterdam in hydrogeology and hydrochemistry. More information on background and experience is available on LinkedIn.

Publications

Written over 50 papers in both peer reviewed journals and professional journals. A complete overview is available on Google Scholar. Most of these are available through ResearchGate.
